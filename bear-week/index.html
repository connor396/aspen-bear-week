The event

A week that puts the whole town behind it.

Businesses, visitors and residents, for one week, funding the restoration work and teaching people how to share a valley with bears.

How it is shaped

Two seasons, two different jobs.

Winter

The week that raises

Aspen is full, and the town has a deep tradition of doing this well. Winter is when the funding for a planting season gets committed.

Spring

The season that plants

Plants go in the ground when they can establish. This is the work the winter week exists to pay for.

Summer

The months that matter

Bears are active and visitors are here. Education runs hardest exactly when the risk is highest.
