Get involved

Most of what we need right now is not money.

Donations are not open yet. These are the things that actually move this forward this month.

If you own land

Talk to us about a site

Private land in the right place, far from housing, is the fastest route to a first planting. No commitment in a first conversation.

If you run a business

Become a founding partner

Display the decal, join the directory, and hand visitors a straight answer about bears. No fee in the first year.

If you have time

Plant, or teach

Planting days need hands. Education needs people who can talk to visitors without lecturing them.

If you live here

Fix your own attractants

Secure the bin, take down the feeder, clear the fruit. It is unglamorous and it is the single highest-return thing any resident can do.
